About Soroti Egg Hub

Soroti Egg Hub brings fresh eggs, sold by the tray, to the people of Soroti City who cook with them every day: rolex and chapati makers, food stalls, shops and households.

What we do

  • One fixed price for every tray of 30 eggs, so you always know what you will pay.
  • Delivery to your area, with a fee that drops as your order grows, or free collection from one of our seven pickup stations.
  • We sell our oldest stock first, so eggs never sit on the shelf.

How ordering works

  1. Choose your trays and add them to your cart.
  2. Pick delivery to your area or free pickup.
  3. Pay with MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, a bank card, or when your eggs arrive.
  4. Follow your order under My orders until it reaches you.
